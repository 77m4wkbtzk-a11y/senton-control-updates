# Senton Control v1.2.9 — Senton Privacy Guard

Status: DEVELOPMENT STARTED — DO NOT PUBLISH

v1.2.9 is the major Privacy Guard release. It must not replace the live v1.2.8 / v1.2.8.1 update-path test until its own implementation and hard tests pass.

## Planned privacy protections

- Detect clearly defined restricted privacy-zone indicators such as toilet/restroom/bathroom/changing-room signage and common male/female restroom symbols.
- Immediately stop camera recording when a restricted privacy-zone indicator is detected.
- Never intentionally record or retain imagery from inside a toilet, restroom, bathroom, shower or changing-room privacy zone.
- Permit at most one sign-only evidence frame captured while still outside the restricted area, only when it contains no nudity/sensitive imagery; bystanders should be blurred before any alert attachment.
- Enter Privacy Lock when restricted-area viewing is detected or when high-risk privacy content is detected.
- Privacy Lock disables camera recording and relevant camera controls until authorised recovery.
- Do not permanently brick Senton Control on a classifier result; use a recoverable privacy lock to account for false positives.
- Log the event without retaining prohibited/private imagery.

## Owner recovery

- The Windows Senton Control app must NOT store or receive the owner's actual unlock PIN.
- Unlock authorisation is intended to live on Senton Link on the owner's phone.
- Senton Link verifies owner authentication locally and sends Senton Control a short-lived authenticated unlock approval.
- PIN values must never appear in logs, emails, update manifests, screenshots or Windows source/configuration.

## Alerts

- Send an owner email when Privacy Lock activates.
- Alert includes device/app identity, time, reason, camera involved and recording-stopped status.
- Do not attach private-area imagery.
- If a compliant sign-only evidence image exists, it may be attached under the evidence policy above.
- Send an unlock/recovery audit notification when appropriate.

## Release gate

Before v1.2.9 can be advertised in update.json:

1. v1.2.8.1 OTA path must be repaired/tested or otherwise superseded by a proven updater path.
2. Source must compile.
3. Privacy Guard unit/hard tests must pass.
4. Existing simulation/failsafe tests must continue to pass.
5. Update cleanup must not delete the active EXE.
6. Release EXE must be built successfully and SHA-256 published.
7. v1.2.9 must not be pushed to the live update channel before all gates pass.
